#anomaly 
## Focus
>Thought or emotion - usually a *feeling* towards a *subject*.
>Quality of the *feeling* can help create the **Impulse**.
>Location of the *subject* often is the location of the **Domain**.
## Impulse
>The anomalies behaviour - with the intent of creating scenarios that lead to the observation of its focus.
## Domain
>Where observations of the **Impulse** are the strongest, sustaining the anomaly and suffusing the objects and location with resonance.
## Appearance
>How the anomaly manifests in our world.
## Current Situation
> What the anomaly is doing now, what has changed?
## History
>Background on the anomaly, how it came into existence, what it has been doing since.
## Special Rule
>Any special rule or ability this anomaly has, and when it triggers. Not all anomalies have these.
## Chaos Effects
> Chaos effects table. Detailing changes for regular chaos effects and how they work for this specific anomaly, and adding any new chaos effects this anomaly has.
## Minor Anomalies
> Details on the kinds of minor anomalies this anomaly creates, if any. And stats for any new or unique minor anomaly types this anomaly creates.
