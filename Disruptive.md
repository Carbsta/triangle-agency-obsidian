Anomalies that amplify the [[Chaos]] happening around them. They often hide as mundane objects, and increased chaos is often the only clue to their existence.

Each time [[Chaos]] is created, **add 1 to the total amount of chaos created per disruptive anomaly nearby.**

Resonants can identify them as Anomalous from physical touch.

Their Threat is applied when destroyed: **they explode, dealing 1 Harm.**
