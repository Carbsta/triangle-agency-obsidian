#anomaly #dead-quiet
## Focus
[[Kaye Renfield]]'s serenity (peace and quiet) achieved through control.
## Impulse
To eliminate any source of noise made during the night in [[Sneinton Market]].
## Domain
The [[sound-proofed basement]] of [[26 Bath Street]] - Kaye's terraced accommodation.
## Appearance
A thin and slender cage of bones. Like the delicate bones of the ear, haphazardly arranging themselves into the human form. From afar appears like a long-limbed skeleton.
A white, featureless head perched on-top of a honeycomb body which is large enough to encase a human within its abdomen. Inside its body no sound can exist.
Its arms and legs have no joints, but hang limply loose from the body like old telephone wires.
Doesn't appear to walk when upright, just appears closer.
On all fours it will bound forward like a wolf - its wiry limbs will elongate and shrink as they hit ground.
## Current Situation
The anomaly started by disrupting and silencing technological sources of sound - using ambient chaos to create [[Wirelings]] from the wires of the objects it destroyed. **At the start of the investigation 10 [[Wirelings]] are active around [[Sneinton Market]].**
It then escalated to remove noise from the source by trapping the people making the noise inside its body. Purging all sound from them, including their heartbeats. Victims die screaming, but no one can hear them.
It has killed three members of the [[Sneinton Market]] community so far:
- [[Billie Lee]] (She/Her) - she was hammering at a metal sculpture installation.
- [[Tony Jackson]] (He/Him) - he was playing the [[Two Jacks]] speaker system at full blast.
- [[Manny Navarro]] (He/Him) - he sang loudly while playing the [[pindrop]] arcade game at [[The Laundrocade]].

**Kaye is unaware of the Anomaly's actions.** She blames the deaths on the resident's "fast living." If only they had listened to her requests for a quieter neighbourhood.
## History
[[Sneinton Market]] is a small close knit community of small businesses and residents. A struggling laundromat revitalised itself by putting arcade machines in the back and calling itself [[The Laundrocade]]. A local bar, [[Two Jacks]], got third place in the city for friendliest service.
An old brick railway building houses a thriving craft market, called [[The Station]].
Recently an article was published in The Times called "Nottingham's Independent Market on the Rise."

[[Kaye Renfield]] was inspired by that article to move to Nottingham and snatch up an affordable terraced house overlooking the market, telling all her friends about her chance to build a life in an "up-and-coming hotspot."
She loves the place - apart from all the noise from the locals:
- [[The Laundrocade]] would be full of teens hanging out at all hours.
- [[Two Jacks]] throws a bass-filled dance party on Friday nights.
- [[The Station]] hosts summer block parties seemingly all the time.
- Locals and students would throw get-togethers late into the night.

She just wanted a single decent nights sleep. She sent emails, cited laws, menaced on the local WhatsApp group, filed complaints with the "proper" authorities. Nobody would listen. At night she would whisper furious pleas into the darkness until one day the Anomaly obeyed.
## Special Rule
**Any singing, playing or performing of music** while in [[Sneinton Market]], outside of the Anomaly's direct presence gives the Anomaly **5 [[Chaos]].**
