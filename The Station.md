#dead-quiet #mission

Convenience Store that was once part of an old victorian bus station.

Sells an eclectic mix of items based on local demands.

Hosts a weekly craft market - leaflets.
Home of many summer block parties - signage in window, leaflets.

Nearby an assortment of metal parts that could possibly be a sculpture have started to rust. Left unfinished when [[Billie Lee]] died.

Shop is hard to navigate.
