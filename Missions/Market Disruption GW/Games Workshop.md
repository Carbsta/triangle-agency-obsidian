Has had a long history of experimenting with and commercialising the anomalous. Their writers have been drawing ideas from the lobby, which they call "the warp" - using it to generate fiction, product ideas, gauge customer sentiment, imbue products with a well timed feeling of zeitgeist and generally maintain a cycle of hype and cult like following of their brand across the globe - all respectable ambitions.
One of the primary reasons the Nottingham branch is one of the largest branch of the region is so we can keep close tabs on our competition.
They are too big to take on directly, and we profit from their activity too, but we are here to prevent them achieving local market dominance.

Insider intel has told us that recently something has gone wrong - perhaps a containment breach? Ripples and other anomalous entities are spilling forth and having unplanned interactions with customers.
So far we are managing the optics by spinning these strange sightings as part of a viral GW marketing campaign, but its only a matter of time until overly curious people see it for what it is.

With the potential for this to spill out in a major way and destabilise the region we are sending in a team to clean up this mess, put a stop to their operations if possible or at the very least get the scoop on their anomalous tech for our R&D department to keep up.
