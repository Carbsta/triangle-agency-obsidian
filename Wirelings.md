When [[The Silence Anomaly]] removes sound from a device, pieces of wire from that device can become Wirelings.

The anomaly cannot create more Wirelings *without* destroying a sound-creating electronic device in the process.

They are drawn towards sources of loud sounds, or people creating [[Chaos]] and constantly report on their location to the Anomaly.

**The Wirelings can be transformed using Manifest/Double/Triple** for **-1 Chaos per Wireling** when the wirelings are **needed to serve a specific purpose:**

- **Electric Wirelings** ([[Violent]]) 2/1
	- Electrocutes those nearby
- **Tangled Wirelings** ([[Imprisoning]]) 0/3
	- wraps and holds anyone within reach
- **Siren Wirelings** ([[Alluring]]) 0/2
	- Draws victims near with beautiful sounds
	- Sounds suddenly become screams loud enough to make ears and noses bleed.
